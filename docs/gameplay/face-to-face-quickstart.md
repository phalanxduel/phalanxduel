# Face-to-Face Quickstart

Use this as the table script for a first Classic v3.0 duel. It is deliberately
short; [How to Play](./how-to-play.md) explains the details and
[Canonical Rules](./rules.md) remain authoritative.

1. **Prepare.** Each player has a separate shuffled 52-card deck and starts at
   20 LP. Draw 12 cards each.
2. **Build.** Make four columns with a front and back rank. P2 deploys first;
   alternate face-up placements until both players have eight battlefield cards
   and four cards in hand.
3. **Start.** P1 attacks first. Only a front-rank card may attack.
4. **Resolve.** Choose an opponent column. Damage travels front card, back
   card, then opponent LP. A surviving card stops the attack; a destroyed card
   lets remaining damage continue.
5. **Respond.** The defender may reinforce only the attacked column. Then the
   defender draws up to four cards and attacks next.
6. **Win.** Reach 0 opponent LP. Passes are limited; an empty deck is not a
   loss.

Remember the suit shorthand: ♦ subtracts between cards, ♥ subtracts before
LP, ♣ doubles once after an eligible destruction, and ♠ doubles into LP.
