<!-- cue:start-soldier -->
Deploy into the front rank and complete your formation. In Classic combat, compare attacker and defender ranks across each lane before you commit.

<!-- cue:pass -->
When you need to keep your formation intact, open the command drawer and press Pass. Repeated passes can cost the match.

<!-- cue:attack -->
Once a lane opens up, eligible reinforcement cards light up in your hand and mark the legal column.

<!-- cue:reinforce -->
Choose a card from your hand and place it into the highlighted opening. The updated formation is visible immediately.
