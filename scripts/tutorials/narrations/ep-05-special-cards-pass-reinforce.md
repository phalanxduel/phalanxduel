<!-- cue:special-intro -->
Welcome to the Season 1 finale of Phalanx Duel! Today we cover the rules that separate beginners from tournament contenders: Aces, Face Cards, Passing, Reinforcing, and Victory Conditions.

<!-- cue:start-soldier -->
Aces and Face Cards follow strict destruction eligibility rules — you can't just brute-force them with any attacker, so placing them in the right column can lock down an entire lane.

<!-- cue:pass-and-reinforce -->
When an attack would put you at a disadvantage, you can Pass to preserve your formation — but watch the pass counter. Whenever a column opens up, the Reinforce phase lets you slide back-row defenders forward or deploy fresh units from your hand.

<!-- cue:victory-conditions -->
A duel ends when a player reaches zero Life Points, forfeits, or triggers the liveness rule. That wraps up Season 1 — in Season 2, we'll sign up your account, unlock custom cardbacks, and customize your HUD!
