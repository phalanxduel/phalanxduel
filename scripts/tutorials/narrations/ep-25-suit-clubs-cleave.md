<!-- cue:clubs-role -->
Clubs are the battering ram of the phalanx. They trigger at the Card-to-Card boundary — right after your Club destroys the opponent's front-rank defender.

<!-- cue:clubs-cleave -->
The excess damage carrying into the back-rank card is doubled once per attack. Aim Clubs at thin front-rank shields to shatter an entire stacked column in a single turn.
