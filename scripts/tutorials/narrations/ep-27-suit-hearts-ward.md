<!-- cue:hearts-role -->
Hearts are your last line of defense. A Heart triggers at the Card-to-Player boundary — only when it is the final card destroyed right before damage reaches your life points.

<!-- cue:hearts-ward -->
Hearts do not stack, and they fire before an attacking Spade doubles overflow. Anchor your back rank with a Heart to neutralize lethal Spade breakthroughs and buy yourself the tempo turn you need to counterattack.
