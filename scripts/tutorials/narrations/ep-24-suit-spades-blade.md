<!-- cue:spades-role -->
Spades are the executioner's blade of Phalanx Duel. Unlike Clubs, Spades do not boost damage between cards — they wait for the final Card-to-Player boundary.

<!-- cue:spades-strike -->
Whenever an attacking Spade breaks through a column and carries damage directly into the opponent's life points, that remaining overflow is doubled. Hold your high Spades for open lanes or wounded columns to close out the match in one lethal strike.
