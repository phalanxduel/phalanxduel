<!-- cue:zero-rng-board -->
Phalanx Duel is built on a single design thesis: zero combat randomness. Once cards hit the two-by-four phalanx, every point of damage is pure geometric consequence — no dice rolls, no critical-hit RNG, and no hidden modifiers.

<!-- cue:column-commitment -->
Each column is a two-rank commitment. Your front rank shields your back rank, and your back rank shields your life points. Deploying a card is both an offense and a promise about which lane you are willing to defend.

<!-- cue:deterministic-witness -->
Because every resolution follows Shield, then Weapon, then Clamp in strict integer arithmetic, you can forecast every strike before you click and verify every outcome afterward in the Combat Math ledger.
