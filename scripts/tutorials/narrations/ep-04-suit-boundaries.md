<!-- cue:suits-intro -->
In Phalanx Duel, suits are not global shields or heals — they are precision combat boundaries that fire only when damage crosses a specific step.

<!-- cue:diamonds-hearts -->
Diamonds defend the card-to-card boundary, absorbing damage before it spills into your back row. Hearts guard the player boundary, softening damage right before it hits your Life Points.

<!-- cue:clubs-spades -->
Clubs and Spades supercharge your offense — amplifying card-to-card breakthroughs and driving extra lethal damage across the column boundary.
