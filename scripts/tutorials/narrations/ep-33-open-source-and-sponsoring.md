<!-- cue:open-source -->
Phalanx Duel is free to play and open source under AGPL-3.0. Every commit across the engine, web client, Go CLI, and site is public history, built in the open as a transparent engineering laboratory.

<!-- cue:sponsor-links -->
Hosting on Fly.io, Postgres on Neon, DNS, email, and tooling are funded out of pocket. If you enjoy playing or exploring the architecture, you can help keep the servers running via GitHub Sponsors, Patreon, or Buy Me a Coffee — always appreciated, never required.
