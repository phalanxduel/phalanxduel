<!-- cue:diamonds-role -->
Diamonds are your column firewall. When a defending Diamond is destroyed in front of another card, its shield triggers at the Card-to-Card boundary.

<!-- cue:diamonds-shield -->
Because Phalanx Duel resolves Shield before Weapon, a Diamond subtracts its value from overflow before an attacking Club can double it. Place Diamonds on the front rank to protect high-value back-rank attackers.
